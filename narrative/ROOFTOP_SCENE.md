# The Rooftop — Scene Seed

**Status:** Confirmed emotional seed. Exact dialogue, blocking, era and placement open.

## Core sequence
A crowded, lively market in an impoverished city district. Elias is occupied with practical concerns and danger; his younger sister insists on showing him something. She leads him through passages and up onto a rooftop. The city opens before them: roofs, distant landmarks, perhaps green country beyond the walls.

He initially sees the risk of the climb. She sees the view. For a moment, he lets himself feel wonder and happiness because she asked him to look.

**Possible line (not final):** 'You never look up, do you?'

## Why it matters
It conveys the siblings' dynamic without exposition, establishes a place of beauty inside hardship, and can later become a painful or restorative memory.
