# Societies, Cities and Power

**Status: Exploring. No specific city or faction canon yet.**

## Promising city contrast

**Confirmed spatial relationship (15 October 2026):** The prosperous-looking upper districts and the inhabited lower neighborhoods are physically connected levels of one enormous city. This does not establish a specific government, noble houses, strict class boundaries or the motives of institutions; those remain open. See [World Overview](WORLD_OVERVIEW.md).
A prosperous center may present itself as safe, civilized and benevolent, with impressive architecture, public ritual and official messaging. People in impoverished districts outside or beneath its privileged spaces face violence and deprivation, but also mutual care and community.

This is a **starting contrast**, not a sufficient plot twist. The city's wealth, political legitimacy, security, and failures must have coherent historical and economic causes.

## Lower-city visual direction — selected reference, not final canon
The first environment pair depicts immense aged arches and viaducts above cramped shelters, market stalls, walkways and warm lights. Poverty is visible, but the district is **alive**: people trade, gather, raise children and look after one another. The presence of vulnerable or homeless children fits the siblings' established community. These are visual studies, not proof of the city's exact engineering, founding history, class system, or the fate of its inhabitants. See the image links in `WORLD_OVERVIEW.md`.

## Moral design rules
- Authority figures can sincerely believe their choices preserve civilization.
- Poor communities are not uniformly virtuous; wealthy communities are not uniformly cruel.
- Institutions contain factions, internal disagreements and conflicting incentives.
- Avoid a generic 'the capital secretly caused everything' reveal without a distinctive causal history.
