# Societies, Cities and Power

**Status:** Confirmed conflict foundations; government, institutions and detailed political systems remain open.

## One connected, unequal city
The upper and lower districts are parts of **one enormous physically connected, vertically layered Gothic city**. Monumental arches, viaducts, foundations and inhabited intermediate levels connect them. Upper districts receive more sunlight and appear wealthier; lower neighborhoods are cramped and dimmer, but full of families, markets, friendships and daily life. These contrasts are confirmed; the city's name, precise map, formal government, class mobility and construction history are not.

## Confirmed conflict — the planned uprising (17 October 2026)
A coercive **lower-city underworld organization** controls parts of the local economy and exploits real grievances against discriminatory upper-city power. Its leaders deliberately plan an armed rebellion to gain control. **It does not represent the majority of lower-city civilians.** The upper-city response and the resulting violence devastate parts of Elias and Mira's home district; not all lower districts are destroyed or abandoned.

Elias has carried out dangerous covert errands for the organization for years to support Mira. An unnamed older childhood friend, who sees the organization as a substitute family, originally introduced him to its work without recognizing its manipulation. The organization holds some form of leverage over Elias (**exact nature open**) and promises a final job will free him. Elias believes he is reopening a restricted upper-city service/smuggling passage; the leaders knowingly use that access to enable the uprising. **Elias does not know he is starting a war.** They regard him as expendable.

Mira is captured among ordinary civilians in the catastrophe and later dies; she is not targeted for secret lineage or destiny. The precise circumstances of her death are open. See [Story Outline](../narrative/STORY_OUTLINE.md) and [Canon Status](../decisions/CANON_STATUS.md).

## Moral and social constraints
- Neither the upper city nor lower city is morally uniform. Individuals and institutions have competing motives.
- Legitimate injustice does not make the organization's coercion and willingness to sacrifice civilians just.
- Before the rebellion, the lower neighborhoods are alive, not an empty dystopian ruin; surviving residents remain afterward.
- Other civilians have stories independent of Elias and Mira. Avoid a simplistic rich-evil/poor-good divide.

## Currency — the Arch
The city's established everyday bronze coin is the **Arch**, bearing a worn architectural arch motif. Its concept artwork is [the Arch coin board](../assets/images/Arches_%20City%20Coin%20Concept%20Sheet.png). The coin and core motif are canon; exact denominations, monetary relationships, minting history and wider economy remain open.

## Still open
Names and internal structure of the organization, its precise economic control and leverage, the upper-city government's composition, policy, military response, religion, class mobility, trade and resource systems, named districts, and exact geography. Visual artwork is not a final city map.
