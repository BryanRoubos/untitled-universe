# World Overview

**Status:** Tone confirmed; geography, metaphysics and historical specifics open.

## Premise
An original, older-feeling dark-fantasy world that can be breathtaking and threatening in the same breath: deep forests, old roads, villages, ruins, castles, powerful cities, divergent cultures, and people trying to build lives amid inherited damage and occasional uncanny events.

A catastrophe or history of disasters may have shaped the world, but the nature and timeline are **not decided**. Some institutions remain durable and powerful. Ordinary social hierarchies and conflicts persist.

## Tone and experience
- Human-scale stories with warmth, moral ambiguity, humor, danger and loss.
- Environments feel inhabited, historically layered and worth examining.
- Mystery is unsettling and evocative rather than constant jump scares.
- Strange events may be real and interactive; their rules remain open.

## Connected vertical city — established spatial direction (15 October 2026)
**Confirmed by the creator:** The grand, sunlit upper districts seen in the approved title art and the densely inhabited lower districts beneath the viaducts exist within **one enormous, physically connected, vertically layered city**. Monumental stone arches, bridges, foundations and intermediate inhabited levels connect the architecture and the lives above and below. This is a spatial relationship, not a claim that all citizens have equal access or status.

The upper levels suggest more monumental, prosperous urban spaces; the lower levels include dense, improvised homes, trade, community and hardship. The lower city is not lifeless or inherently morally inferior, and the upper city is not uniformly villainous.

**Visual and social progression (clarified 17 October 2026):** The city should read as a continuous ascent: especially crowded and neglected deepest districts; inhabited, working and market levels; monumental viaducts and intermediate settlements; and increasingly open, sunlit upper districts. These are broad patterns of space, infrastructure and opportunity, **not** a rigid wealth ladder or a moral division. Wealth, hardship, influence, kindness and exploitation can exist at any elevation. The lower districts retain commerce, community and ordinary life. In images, natural light follows the architecture: the viaducts cast large shadows, openings reveal pockets of daylight, and upper structures receive more direct sun. Avoid a uniformly black lower city or an artificially glowing band of markets. These are spatial and visual guidelines, not a finalized map, class system or construction history. The *Arcane* comparison is an inspiration for visual storytelling and social complexity, **not** permission to copy Piltover, Zaun, factions or plot.

## First environment exploration — the lower city (15 October 2026)
The creator selected an atmospheric concept of a poor but lively lower-city market under vast, ancient-looking arches and bridges, together with a simple monochrome architectural blockout. The scale, vertical layering, improvised housing, lantern-lit trade and human warmth are **approved visual inspiration**, not finalized geography or lore. Elias and his sister grew up among vulnerable children in an impoverished urban community; the precise layout of their neighborhood remains open, while its connection to the upper districts is now confirmed.

## Children's hidden shelter — environment exploration (15 October 2026)
The creator selected a **concealed, cold, enclosed refuge** as a promising visual setting for the children who grew up in the lower city. They have made the damp stone chamber livable with makeshift beds, crates, blankets and candlelight, but it remains sad and uncomfortable. The design study shows a rubble-concealed child-sized entrance in a neglected storage alcove, a low passage with a turn, and a small high vent rather than an exposed opening onto the city.

- [Grayscale hidden-shelter storyboard and blockout](../assets/images/Hidden%20Children%E2%80%99s%20Shelter%20Storyboard.png)
- **Status:** Approved environment concept direction, **not** final geography, construction plan or story canon. No decision yet on the shelter's origin, occupants, exact position, or connection to the sister's disappearance.

## Exploration example — not canon
In a ruined house, Elias hears piano music and meets an old woman who offers tea. Later, a photograph suggests she died decades earlier in a fire. Returning reveals ash, yet her cup remains in his bag. The value is the emotional ambiguity and physical trace, not a preselected explanation.

## Open worldbuilding questions
What happened to this world? How do people explain anomalies? How do technologies, travel, faith and power interact? What is safe, and for whom?

## Approved atmospheric city concept — 17 October 2026

Reference: [Lone Adventurer Overlooking the Twilight Citadel](../Lone%20Adventurer%20Overlooking%20the%20Twilight%20Citadel.png) (concept art, not a final map). Creator-approved **visual direction**: a massive connected vertical city with dramatic sunlight on the upper districts, much dimmer lower neighborhoods, damp and dirty masonry, moss, weathering, grime and pockets of warm artificial light. The contrast is beautiful from afar and unpleasant up close. The seated figure's filthy rooftop is a strong material reference.

**Not canonized from the image:** exact buildings, bridges, proportions, spatial openness, population density or an empty undercity. The concept currently feels too open and lacks people. Future street-level studies should show cramped, densely layered, inhabited lower streets and **lively markets**, traders, crowds, stalls and areas with very little visible sky. Preserve the city's life as well as its neglect.
