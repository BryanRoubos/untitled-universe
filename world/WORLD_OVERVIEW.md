# World Overview — Current Reference

> **Author reference; may contain story spoilers.** Current canon is defined by [CANON_STATUS.md](../decisions/CANON_STATUS.md). Historical drafts are in [the archive](../archive/2026-10-17/). This document describes the world, not a finished map.

## Established geography and daily life

The setting contains **one enormous, physically connected, vertically layered Gothic city**. Monumental viaducts, bridges, stairways and inhabited intermediate levels connect the bright upper districts to crowded lower neighborhoods. The upper levels tend to receive more sunlight and offer greater wealth and influence; the lower districts contain dense housing, workshops, trade, ordinary families, community, and hardship. These are tendencies, **not** a rigid moral or economic hierarchy. No elevation is uniformly virtuous or corrupt.

The city is structurally plausible: arches bear weight, passages lead somewhere, and light follows openings and shadows rather than arbitrary glowing bands. The lower city remains populated and alive. The uprising damages **parts** of it; other districts continue to function. Some upper-city windows remain illuminated after the conflict, without implying that all upper districts escaped harm.

Elias and Mira **lived together** before the uprising in a concealed stone-vaulted shelter inside the foundations of the great viaducts. The shelter storyboard explores a storage-alcove approach, displaced stones and a crawlable passage. The illustrated candlelit chamber is approved visual direction. Neither illustration fixes the exact floor plan, objects, address or passage dimensions; any entrance must remain usable by adult Elias.

## Established social and story context — author spoilers

A coercive lower-city underworld faction controls portions of the local economy and exploits real grievances about inequality. Its leaders knowingly plan an armed uprising for greater control; they do not represent the lower-city population. Elias has undertaken covert work for them under leverage. They exploit him to open a restricted passage as his promised final assignment, concealing the intended military use. Upper-city retaliation and fighting damage civilian neighborhoods. Ordinary civilians are not treated as expendable background characters.

The city uses everyday bronze coins called **Arch**, featuring an architectural arch motif. **Kareth** is a month name. No denominations, wider calendar or institutions are established.

## Approved visual direction (not a map)

A tactile, hand-painted Gothic-fantasy aesthetic: aged ochre and charcoal, cool deep-city shadow, small warm lights, imperfect masonry, grime, moss, rough brush texture and occasional parchment edges. The journal spread and Elias writing at dusk are master style references. The panoramic vertical-city art is conceptual geography; the market studies and ruined district are individual environment concepts, not evidence that all streets share those layouts.

## Open questions

The city's name, exact map, history, construction methods, government, class institutions, access restrictions, religious structures, other cultures and settlements, and the details of the faction's organization remain undecided. Supernatural mechanisms and wider world history are exploratory; do not convert image labels or early ideas into facts.

## Retired or nonbinding directions

The modern/post-apocalyptic direction is superseded by historical-feeling dark fantasy. Comparisons to *Arcane* and *Metro* are influences, not imported lore. A fully deserted lower city, an exact city map inferred from concept art, and a child-only shelter entrance are **not** established. Older explorations are preserved in the [dated archive](../archive/2026-10-17/WORLD_OVERVIEW_previous.md).
