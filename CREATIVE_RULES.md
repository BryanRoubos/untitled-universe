# Creative Rules

**Status: Confirmed collaboration principles**

1. **Universe first; story before medium.** Develop the story in prose first, without assuming it is a game. Let the characters, emotional truth, and narrative determine what form or adaptation suits it later. Do not let gameplay requirements prematurely constrain the story; only shift into game design when the creator explicitly chooses to.
2. **Creator decides canon.** Assistant suggestions never become confirmed automatically.
3. **Label uncertainty.** Use Confirmed / Working / Exploring / Open / Rejected. Avoid presenting speculation as history.
4. **Characters are people.** Nuanced motives, contradiction, humor, kindness, conflict and agency. No single-trait villains or plot-device companions.
5. **Show, don't diagnose.** Let trauma and depression appear in habits, actions, relationships, and choices rather than speeches.
6. **Darkness needs contrast.** Beauty, wonder, intimacy, humor and hope matter as much as danger and loss.
7. **Originality over imitation.** Inspirations are emotional and structural touchstones, not lore or visual templates to copy.
8. **Meaningful places.** Environmental storytelling and discovery should reward attention.
9. **Small iterative sessions.** Produce one tangible result at a time. Alternate writing, art, modeling and prototyping as motivation allows.
10. **Quality over scope.** Favor a small polished experience over a vast unfinished one.
11. **Critique weak ideas.** Challenge generic premises, easy morality, implausible motivations and unexplained world rules.
12. **Keep the archive current.** Record new approvals, open questions, superseded ideas and links to assets.

## Inspiration, not canon
The Witcher 3 (moral quests, exploration), The Last of Us (relationships, animation), Metro (atmosphere), Twilight Princess (eerie fantasy), Andor (political humanity), Planet of the Apes (civilizations), and street-child stories from A Knight of the Seven Kingdoms.
