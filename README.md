> **Canon update — 17 October 2026:** Mira Varek died at age 15. Elias was approximately 21 then and is approximately 23 at the start of the main story, around two years later. The audience initially believes Mira is alive and follows Elias's search. The narrative opens in the present and interweaves earlier scenes, including Mira's independent perspective. Elias's recollections can be unreliable; subtle, fair clues accumulate toward a revelation that recontextualizes earlier scenes. The actual circumstances of Mira's death, psychological mechanism, exact clues, and dialogue remain open. The rooftop birthday event and her death are separate: in reality the siblings leave the rooftop together, while Elias later recalls her leaving alone. Earlier contradictory claims in this file are superseded by this update.

## Project overview

*Where the Light Falls* is the **creator-approved title of the first game** set in a larger original fantasy world. The wider universe has **no confirmed name**; `untitled-universe` remains the repository name.

The story follows **Elias Varek** (working name) and the disappearance of his younger sister **Mira Varek**, who was **17** when she disappeared. Their relationship is the emotional foundation; the circumstances and outcome remain open.

### The city

The monumental, sunlit upper districts and the inhabited lower districts form **one physically connected, vertically layered city**. Vast stone arches, bridges and intermediate levels connect the two. The exact map, city name, political system and history are **not yet canon**.

## Approved atmospheric city concept — 17 October 2026

Reference: [Lone Adventurer Overlooking the Twilight Citadel](Lone%20Adventurer%20Overlooking%20the%20Twilight%20Citadel.png) (concept art, not a final map). Creator-approved **visual direction**: a massive connected vertical city with dramatic sunlight on the upper districts, much dimmer lower neighborhoods, damp and dirty masonry, moss, weathering, grime and pockets of warm artificial light. The contrast is beautiful from afar and unpleasant up close. The seated figure's filthy rooftop is a strong material reference.

**Not canonized from the image:** exact buildings, bridges, proportions, spatial openness, population density or an empty undercity. The concept currently feels too open and lacks people. Future street-level studies should show cramped, densely layered, inhabited lower streets and **lively markets**, traders, crowds, stalls and areas with very little visible sky. Preserve the city's life as well as its neglect.
