## Project overview

*Where the Light Falls* is the **creator-approved title for the central story project** set in a larger original fantasy world. The project's medium and final form remain deliberately undecided. The wider universe has **no confirmed name**; `untitled-universe` remains the repository name.

The story follows **Elias Varek** (working name) and the disappearance of his younger sister **Mira Varek**, who died at **15** about two years before the present. Elias believes she is missing; the audience discovers the truth alongside him near the ending.

### The city

The monumental, sunlit upper districts and the inhabited lower districts form **one physically connected, vertically layered city**. Vast stone arches, bridges and intermediate levels connect the two. The exact map, city name, political system and history are **not yet canon**.

## Approved atmospheric city concept — 17 October 2026

Reference: [Lone Adventurer Overlooking the Twilight Citadel](assets/images/Lone%20Adventurer%20Overlooking%20the%20Twilight%20Citadel.png) (concept art, not a final map). Creator-approved **visual direction**: a massive connected vertical city with dramatic sunlight on the upper districts, much dimmer lower neighborhoods, damp and dirty masonry, moss, weathering, grime and pockets of warm artificial light. The contrast is beautiful from afar and unpleasant up close. The seated figure's filthy rooftop is a strong material reference.

**Not canonized from the image:** exact buildings, bridges, proportions, spatial openness, population density or an empty undercity. The concept currently feels too open and lacks people. Future street-level studies should show cramped, densely layered, inhabited lower streets and **lively markets**, traders, crowds, stalls and areas with very little visible sky. Preserve the city's life as well as its neglect.

## Journal canon — 17 October 2026

Elias keeps a deeply private journal. Mira secretly took and read it while she was alive and left a small drawing or message. He discovers this only **after the uprising**. The exact pages, physical design and discovery scene remain open; the journal is a relationship detail, not a solution to the central mystery. See [Elias's Personal Items](characters/ELIAS_VAREK_ITEMS.md).

## Journal concept artwork — approved 17 October 2026

[View the journal concept](assets/images/Elias%20and%20Mira%E2%80%99s%20Worn%20Journal%20Spread.png). **Approved visual concept (17 October 2026):** Weathered leather journal, uneven parchment, muted ochre and charcoal, painterly graphite/sketchbook style rather than photorealism. Elias's handwriting is neat, restrained and contained; Mira's is much sloppier, playful and irregular with doodles and corrections. The depicted exact text, dates, names, drawings and page arrangement are exploratory, NOT literal canon. The secret reading and later discovery remain confirmed story events.

## Calendar note — Kareth

**Kareth** is a confirmed month name used in the city's calendar. The journal concept's “17th of Kareth” is an acceptable example date, **not** confirmation of an event on that day. Its season, order in the year, number of days, origin, other month names, and wider calendar structure remain **open**.
