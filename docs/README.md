## Editorial rules

1. Check the canon tracker before writing. Do not elevate exploration to canon.
2. Put reader-facing language in the website; put statuses, production decisions and spoilers in the Workshop or author documents.
3. Keep spoilers out of public-facing prose, captions and open Workshop cards.
4. Record explicit approvals in the decision log, then update the canon tracker and relevant topical document.
5. Preserve superseded text in dated archives rather than stacking correction headers above contradictory drafts.
6. Keep image filenames stable to avoid broken links. Artwork does not establish incidental lore.
