CHAPTER I / WHERE THE LIGHT FALLS

A City in Layers

A single city stretches toward the light while whole neighborhoods live beneath its shadow.

The city rises in layers. Bridges cross impossible distances between towers, roofs become roads, and entire neighborhoods gather beneath the weight of stone. To live here is to know that a short distance on a map can mean an hour of climbing.

High above, the wealthier districts receive open sky and sunlight. Far below, the streets are narrower and the daylight arrives in fragments. Yet the lower city is not merely darkness or ruin. It has markets, music, crowded homes, children, arguments, work, laughter and a thousand ordinary routines.

Its districts belong to one connected place, not separate worlds. Passages, lifts, stairs and viaducts join people who may never understand one another.

From the studio

The monochrome sketch and finished textured interpretation show the same lower-city environment at two stages of development, not two separate locations. They establish atmosphere and architectural direction, not an approved map. The city’s name, government and detailed history remain undecided.

CHAPTER II / WHERE THE LIGHT FALLS

Elias Varek

A young man who learned to keep someone else alive before he learned how to live for himself.

Elias knows the city by the routes that are not drawn. He remembers where a guard will look, which wall can be climbed and which roofs are safe after rain. These are not gifts granted by fate. They are skills learned through years of surviving.

At approximately twenty-three, he is attentive to other people’s suffering but struggles to accept their care in return. He can be gentle, perceptive and quietly funny. He can also disappear for days, break promises and push away those who try to get close.

He raised his younger sister, Mira, in the absence of their parents. Responsibility gave him purpose, but it also became a way of measuring his own worth. Protecting her could turn into controlling her. His love was real; so were the limits it placed on them both.

On Mira’s birthday, she takes Elias to a rooftop overlooking their city. They argue about his secrecy, his protectiveness and her need to make choices for herself. They leave together, but the disagreement remains unresolved.

Later, an uprising tears through the city. Mira is taken with other civilians, and Elias cannot reach her. Now he searches for his sister, holding on to the possibility that she survived. His depression and emotional struggles are not caused solely by her disappearance; the search is nevertheless his only reason to keep going.

⚠ Story spoiler · Elias’s hidden role
Confirmed story truth · reveals major plot information

Elias unknowingly helped make the uprising possible. For years he carried out covert jobs for the coercive lower-city faction. On a promised final assignment, he reopened a restricted upper-city passage, believing it was for clandestine passage or smuggling. The faction secretly intended to use that route to launch its attack. Elias did not knowingly choose to start the uprising.

Personal Artifacts

The shared pendant. The pendant came from Mira, and both siblings wear it. Eventually, it is the only thing Elias finds and has from her. Its connection to Mira is confirmed; how she obtained it, where it originated, and how Elias finds it remain open questions.

The engraved silver ring. Retained as a documented personal item. Its history and significance have not been established here.

The weathered leather journal. Retained as a documented personal item. Its contents and history remain open.

These objects are part of Elias’s character documentation. Their unresolved details are intentionally left unanswered rather than filled with invented symbolism.

Designing Elias

The clothing is practical and worn: a dirty-cream linen shirt, faded charcoal vest, gray-brown sash, dark trousers and weathered boots. The materials tell a story of repeated use, not heroic costume.

CHAPTER III / WHERE THE LIGHT FALLS

Mira Varek

Not a symbol, a prophecy, or a prize to be recovered. A person with a life of her own.

Mira grew up with a brother who tried to give her everything he could, even when there was little to give. She understood more of his exhaustion than he realized. She loved him and could tell him so plainly, while he often had no idea what to do with the words.

But being loved so fiercely can become its own kind of confinement. Mira wanted room to make mistakes, ask questions and discover the city for herself. She did not want to be responsible for keeping Elias happy.

They disagreed. They hurt one another without intending to. Neither was wrong to need what the other struggled to provide.

Her place in the story is not determined by secret bloodlines or extraordinary powers. Her ordinary hopes and choices matter because she is human.

When violence breaks out across the city, Mira is among the civilians taken captive. Elias sees her being taken but cannot reach her. What happens after that remains the central question of his search.

⚠ Ending spoiler · Mira’s fate
Confirmed story truth · reveals major plot information

Mira died at fifteen, roughly two years before the present story. Elias believes she may still be alive. The audience discovers the truth with him near the ending, not at the beginning.

She was taken with other civilians during the uprising, survived the initial capture and died later. Elias never witnessed her death. Her exact circumstances remain undecided; they are not established canon.

Character in development

Mira’s final portrait, clothing and many details of her independent life are not yet approved. These studies are presented as explorations, not settled canon.

CHAPTER IV / WHERE THE LIGHT FALLS

Lives Beneath the Arches

A city is made of the people who wake up in it each morning.

Before the streets became associated with catastrophe, they were simply home. Vendors argued over prices, neighbors traded favors, and children learned which alleys led to a friend’s door.

Life here is shaped by inequality, but its people are not defined solely by hardship. Nor is the upper city inhabited only by villains. The divisions are structural and political, lived through countless individual choices.

In the lower districts, an organized faction gains influence through work, supplies, debt and intimidation. It draws power from real grievances while pursuing its own interests. Most residents are not part of it. Most want to get through another day in peace.

Unfinished worldbuilding

The faction, city districts and political institutions do not yet have final names. Their motivations are being developed without making entire social groups uniformly good or evil.

CHAPTER V / WHERE THE LIGHT FALLS

The Search

The story begins with a question Elias cannot bear to leave unanswered.

After the uprising, parts of the once-busy city lie damaged and scarred by violence. Yet beyond the ruined streets, other neighborhoods endure. People rebuild what they can, mourn those they have lost, and try to find a way forward in a city that no longer feels quite like home.

Elias moves through a city scarred by the uprising. During the violence, he glimpsed Mira among civilians being taken away and was unable to save her. He does not know what became of her afterward, and he believes she may still be somewhere beyond his reach.

His journey is not about becoming a chosen hero. It is about memory, responsibility, the damage people do while trying to protect one another, and the possibility of a life beyond a single purpose.

The narrative will move between the present search and moments from the past, including Mira’s own perspective. Not every answer is ready to be told. Some are deliberately being saved for the story itself.

⚠ Story spoiler · Who enabled the uprising
Confirmed story truth · reveals major plot information

The faction deliberately used Elias to enable the uprising. Its leaders promised to release him from their control after one last assignment, while concealing that the passage he opened would be used for their planned attack. His unwitting involvement is a major source of guilt and a revelation about the catastrophe.

Ending revelation: Mira is already dead during Elias’s present-day search. The story must offer fair clues without falsely presenting her past perspective. The exact events of her death and the final scenes are still being developed.

Some things remain with us long after the light has moved.
Story status

The emotional foundation and central mystery are established. The full sequence of Elias’s journey, supporting cast and narrative scenes are still being written. This public edition avoids revealing the ending.

CHAPTER VI / WHERE THE LIGHT FALLS

The Artist’s Table

A record of the world as it takes shape.

This is a living manuscript. Some designs are approved; others are questions rendered in pencil and light. We keep both, but distinguish them clearly.

The world’s foundations—its story, characters, environments and creative ideas—come from Bryan Roubos. As a solo creator, Bryan begins with his own pencil drawings, sketches and ideas, then uses AI-assisted concept studies to explore and refine them into more detailed sketches and artwork. This process helps visualize possibilities and develop the project without spending months producing every exploratory image by hand. AI is a tool in that process, not the source of the project’s creative vision; individual studies remain concepts unless explicitly approved.

THE WORKSHOP / CREATOR'S NOTES

The Workshop

The questions we are still asking, kept separate from the story we have already decided.

This is the working table behind Where the Light Falls. Ideas listed here are not canon until deliberately approved. The illustrated chapters above remain the reading experience; major confirmed revelations are optional red spoiler panels in their relevant chapters.

In development · Narrative

What happens after capture?

Mira's exact death circumstances, the timing of the revelation, Elias's response, and the fair clues that lead there still need to be written.

In development · Characters

The childhood friend

The trusting friend who introduced Elias to the faction has no final name, fate or complete character arc yet.

In development · World

The city and its powers

The city's name, government, faction name, detailed political structures, and precise geography are open. The upper and lower districts are one connected city.

In development · Mystery

Memory and the uncanny

Unreliable memories are a possible future idea, not current canon. Revisiting real events with additional context is an option; supernatural elements remain undecided.

In development · Design

Mira's final look

Her portrait studies are exploratory. Her final facial design, clothing, and many details of her independent daily life remain open.

In development · Format

Finding the right medium

The project's medium and final form remain deliberately open. We are developing the world, characters and story without presuming a game, book, or any other format. No medium-specific production, gameplay systems, or book structure is currently a commitment.

This workshop is public, not password-protected. Canon spoilers are hidden by default for casual readers, but the website and linked public GitHub repository are not secure storage for confidential story material.
