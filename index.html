CHAPTER I / WHERE THE LIGHT FALLS

A City in Layers

A single city stretches toward the light while whole neighborhoods live beneath its shadow.

The city rises in layers. Bridges cross impossible distances between towers, roofs become roads, and entire neighborhoods gather beneath the weight of stone. To live here is to know that a short distance on a map can mean an hour of climbing.

High above, the wealthier districts receive open sky and sunlight. Far below, the streets are narrower and the daylight arrives in fragments. Yet the lower city is not merely darkness or ruin. It has markets, music, crowded homes, children, arguments, work, laughter and a thousand ordinary routines.

Its districts belong to one connected place, not separate worlds. Passages, lifts, stairs and viaducts join people who may never understand one another.

From the studio

The monochrome sketch and finished textured interpretation show the same lower-city environment at two stages of development, not two separate locations. They establish atmosphere and architectural direction, not an approved map. The city’s name, government and detailed history remain undecided.

CHAPTER II / WHERE THE LIGHT FALLS

Elias Varek

A young man who learned to keep someone else alive before he learned how to live for himself.

Elias knows the city by the routes that are not drawn. He remembers where a guard will look, which wall can be climbed and which roofs are safe after rain. These are not gifts granted by fate. They are skills learned through years of surviving.

At approximately twenty-three, he is attentive to other people’s suffering but struggles to accept their care in return. He can be gentle, perceptive and quietly funny. He can also disappear for days, break promises and push away those who try to get close.

He raised his younger sister, Mira, in the absence of their parents. Responsibility gave him purpose, but it also became a way of measuring his own worth. Protecting her could turn into controlling her. His love was real; so were the limits it placed on them both.

Now he searches for Mira. What happened to her is the question he cannot stop asking.

CHAPTER III / WHERE THE LIGHT FALLS

Mira Varek

Not a symbol, a prophecy, or a prize to be recovered. A person with a life of her own.

Mira grew up with a brother who tried to give her everything he could, even when there was little to give. She understood more of his exhaustion than he realized. She loved him and could tell him so plainly, while he often had no idea what to do with the words.

But being loved so fiercely can become its own kind of confinement. Mira wanted room to make mistakes, ask questions and discover the city for herself. She did not want to be responsible for keeping Elias happy.

They disagreed. They hurt one another without intending to. Neither was wrong to need what the other struggled to provide.

Her place in the story is not determined by secret bloodlines or extraordinary powers. Her ordinary hopes and choices matter because she is human.

Character in development

Mira’s final portrait, clothing and many details of her independent life are not yet approved. These studies are presented as explorations, not settled canon.

CHAPTER IV / WHERE THE LIGHT FALLS

Lives Beneath the Arches

A city is made of the people who wake up in it each morning.

Before the streets became associated with catastrophe, they were simply home. Vendors argued over prices, neighbors traded favors, and children learned which alleys led to a friend’s door.

Life here is shaped by inequality, but its people are not defined solely by hardship. Nor is the upper city inhabited only by villains. The divisions are structural and political, lived through countless individual choices.

In the lower districts, an organized faction gains influence through work, supplies, debt and intimidation. It draws power from real grievances while pursuing its own interests. Most residents are not part of it. Most want to get through another day in peace.

Unfinished worldbuilding

The faction, city districts and political institutions do not yet have final names. Their motivations are being developed without making entire social groups uniformly good or evil.

CHAPTER V / WHERE THE LIGHT FALLS

The Search

The story begins with a question Elias cannot bear to leave unanswered.

Elias moves through a city whose streets are marked by events he struggles to piece together. He knows that Mira was caught in a period of terrible violence. He believes she may still be somewhere beyond his reach.

His journey is not about becoming a chosen hero. It is about memory, responsibility, the damage people do while trying to protect one another, and the possibility of a life beyond a single purpose.

The narrative will move between the present search and moments from the past, including Mira’s own perspective. Not every answer is ready to be told. Some are deliberately being saved for the story itself.

Some things remain with us long after the light has moved.
Story status

The emotional foundation and central mystery are established. The full sequence of Elias’s journey, supporting cast and narrative scenes are still being written. This public edition avoids revealing the ending.

The Artist’s Table
