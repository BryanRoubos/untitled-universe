Character dossier / Protagonist

Elias Varek

Core confirmedDesign in progress

A young man who learned to protect someone else long before he learned how to live for himself.

Compassionate, vigilant and quietly exhausted, Elias carries the weight of his sister's disappearance. His grief does not define every part of him: he still has humor, tenderness and an instinct to help others.

Character development / Costume study02 / ELIAS

Clothing, worn
and repaired.

Elias wears practical layers shaped by necessity: a dirty-cream linen shirt, faded charcoal vest, gray-brown sash, weathered dark-brown trousers and worn leather boots. These studies trace the outfit from a first graphite sketch to a colored fabric and texture exploration.

Costume design approved as a working reference. Facial design and personal objects are documented separately; all illustrations are AI-assisted concept art.

Narrative / Scene study 00104—06

The rooftop.

Beyond the rooftopsKeyframe illustration pending

A busy market. A reluctant older brother. A younger sister determined to show him something beautiful.

She draws Elias through the streets and up to a rooftop. For one rare moment, he stops watching for threats and sees the wider world through her eyes.

It is an emotional anchor, not yet a finalized script or a confirmed playable opening.
