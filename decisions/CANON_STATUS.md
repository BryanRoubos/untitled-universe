# Canon Status — Living Tracker

## Confirmed creative direction
- **Where the Light Falls** is the creator-approved title of the first game, centered on Elias and Mira Varek.
- The approved title artwork is `Where the Light Falls_ Citadel of Gold.png` (15 October 2026), published in the website hero.
- The wider universe has **no confirmed name**. **The Hollow Crown** was discussed but set aside because of existing media and game-name overlap.
- **UNTITLED** remains the repository's historical working name.
- The universe matters beyond its first game adaptation.
- The story centers on **Elias Varek** (working name) and the unresolved disappearance of his 17-year-old younger sister, **Mira Varek**.
- Elias is a compassionate but deeply traumatized young man; his sister was once his reason to keep living.
- He grew up protecting/raising his sister in a dangerous impoverished urban community, amid moments of friendship and warmth.
- Their contrasting personalities and a rooftop memory are central emotional anchors.
- Exploration should be dense, handcrafted, and story-rich rather than huge and empty.
- Storytelling should be mature, nuanced, atmospheric, sometimes supernatural, and not relentlessly bleak.
- Progress should be iterative and achievable by a solo creator.

- **Elias facial design: Concept 03 approved (15 October 2026).** See `characters/ELIAS_VAREK.md`. Colored study and rough pencil sketch exist but are not yet uploaded as image assets.

- **Elias clothing direction and palette approved (15 October 2026).** Graphite outfit sketch and colored material board are archived in the repository and shown in the concept book; see `characters/ELIAS_VAREK.md`.

## Mira Varek — confirmed identity and facial design exploration (15 October 2026)
- **Confirmed canon:** Mira Varek is Elias's younger sister, age **17 at her disappearance**. Elias is approximately 23; their age gap is approximately six years.
- Two uploaded graphite portrait studies are archived: `Graphite Portrait Studies on Aged Paper.png` (front and side) and `Graphite Portrait Study Sheet.png` (multi-angle and facial details).
- They explore a freckled youthful appearance, untidy dark hair tied back, and rough pencil-on-paper draftsmanship matching Elias's sketchbook approach.
- **Status:** exploratory artwork uploaded and published, **not yet final canon**. Her name and age are confirmed; final appearance, clothing and fate remain open. The sibling relationship and her independent personality remain the confirmed foundations.

## Selected environment visual direction (15 October 2026)
- **Lower city:** Creator approved the visual direction of a vast, old, poor but alive neighborhood beneath monumental stone arches and viaducts. A grayscale blockout and colored atmosphere study are saved in the repo and displayed in the concept book.
- **Status:** Visual reference approved; precise geography, construction history, civic structures and how this setting connects to the sibling story remain exploratory, not canon.

## Hidden shelter — selected visual exploration (15 October 2026)
- The creator likes a **secret, cold, damp children's refuge** within the lower city's massive stonework, made modestly homelike with scavenged bedding, candles and small personal details. It should be enclosed and difficult to discover, not openly visible from bridges or public walkways.
- The uploaded grayscale storyboard explores an ordinary-looking storage alcove, loose stones covering a child-sized crawlspace, a narrow passage and bend, an enclosed chamber, and a small high vent. The artwork is archived and published in the concept book.
- **Status:** Approved *visual direction only*. Precise site, occupants, history, architecture and narrative use remain open; do not assume the sister disappeared here or that this is a locked game level.

## Working / strongly favored
- Older, historical-feeling **dark fantasy** rather than a modern-gun post-apocalypse; exact technology undecided.
- The siblings' serious argument, Elias choosing not to follow, and her disappearance during a subsequent violent event.
- Powerful stable institutions/cities alongside ruined and dangerous regions.
- Elias's previously discussed appearance and outfit (see character document) are an approved visual baseline, but setting-compatible revisions may be needed.

## Strong narrative possibility — pendant (not canon)
- Elias's existing small circular silver pendant may originally have belonged to his sister and may be the only physical trace of her he found after she disappeared. The creator likes this direction, but it is **not confirmed canon**.
- The argument, disappearance scene, aftermath, where/how the pendant was found, and its exact engraving or meaning remain unwritten and open. The star-and-crescent design is exploratory only. The parents' whereabouts and fate are unknown; do not infer them from the pendant.

## Exploring, not canon
- Reality/time/memory fractures as an explanation for eerie encounters.
- A city with an official narrative of safety and prosperity that conceals or rationalizes suffering.
- Religious groups, cults or rival interpretations of anomalies.
- Specific ghostly encounters, including the tea-cup story, as potential quests.
- A playable childhood rooftop memory as prologue or vertical slice.

## Open questions
- Wider-universe name, map, calendar, technology level, and history of the world.
- Nature, rules and frequency of the supernatural.
- City name, geography, class structure and institutional motives.
- Parents' whereabouts and fate; Mira's individual aspirations, agency, and ultimate fate.
- Exact violent incident and surviving childhood friends.
- Main plot beyond the sister search, factions and consequences.
- How the current modern-inspired Elias outfit fits the older fantasy direction.
- Exact game genre mechanics, combat and playable opening.

## Rejected or superseded directions
- A strictly modern Metro-style post-apocalyptic setting is no longer the leading direction.
- Generic rich-evil/poor-good politics, monsters without purpose, or a simplistic 'city hides the truth' twist are insufficient.
- A giant open world or a long rigid production plan is not the preferred approach.
