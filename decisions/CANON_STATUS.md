# Canon Status — Authoritative Current Reference

**Updated:** 17 October 2026

## 1. Confirmed story canon

- **Project:** *Where the Light Falls* is the approved title of the central story. Its final medium is not decided.
- **Characters and ages:** Elias Varek is about 23 in the present; Mira Varek was 15 at death, about two years earlier, when Elias was about 21. He effectively raised her. Their love is genuine, but his protectiveness sometimes restricts her independence.
- **Home:** Before the uprising, Elias and Mira lived in a concealed vaulted shelter within the great viaducts' stone foundations. It was their home, not an occasional refuge.
- **Mira’s birthday rooftop:** It is Mira’s birthday. She persuades a reluctant Elias to accompany her because it is her birthday, and shows him a beautiful city view. They argue about secrecy, his protectiveness and her autonomy. **Both leave together**; she does not die there.
- **Faction:** A coercive lower-city organization exploits genuine inequality, controls parts of the local economy and plans armed uprising for its leaders' power. It does not represent all lower-city residents.
- **Elias's work:** He has performed dangerous covert errands under leverage. A trusting, older childhood friend introduced him to the group without understanding its manipulation. The group promises freedom after a final job.
- **Final job:** Elias opens a restricted upper-city passage believing it serves clandestine movement, unaware that faction leaders plan to use it for an attack. He does not knowingly choose to begin the uprising.
- **Capture and fate — ending spoiler:** Mira is taken among ordinary civilians in the uprising, survives the initial chaos and dies later. Elias witnesses the capture but **not her death**. He searches for her believing she might live. **Elias and the audience learn of her death together near the ending**, with room afterward for his response.
- **Objects:** Mira owned the silver pendant Elias carries after her disappearance; it is his only remaining keepsake of her. Elias keeps a private journal; Mira secretly read it and left a drawing or message. He discovers this only after she disappears. **Arch** is an everyday bronze coin with an arch motif. **Kareth** is a month name.
- **City:** One enormous physically connected, vertically layered Gothic city. Upper levels are generally brighter and wealthier, lower neighborhoods denser and shadowed but lively. The uprising damages some districts, not all. People at every level have ordinary lives and mixed motives.

## 2. Approved visual direction — not literal plot canon

- **Master style:** Tactile hand-painted Gothic fantasy; aged paper, rough strokes, ochre-gold and charcoal, believable architecture and light. Journal spread and *The Wayfarer's Journal at Dusk* are style references.
- **Elias:** Concept 03 face and *Elias Clothing Concept Board* historical-feeling layered costume approved. Earlier hoodie/jeans direction superseded.
- **Mira:** Graphite portraits remain exploratory, **not** her final approved face.
- **Home:** *Candlelit Medieval Stone Chamber* approved as a lived-in home illustration; the shelter storyboard guides concealed access and vaulted form. Furniture placement, possessions and floor plan are not binding.
- **City and objects:** Vertical-city overview, lower market, post-uprising Silent Market, Arch coin and rooftop paintings guide atmosphere and visual design. They are **not** fixed maps, proof of universal destruction, exact scene dates or automatic worldbuilding facts.
- **Journal:** Leather/parchment mood and contrast between Elias's restrained handwriting and Mira's playful marks are approved. Exact page text, date and drawing are not.

## 4. Retired / rejected as current canon

- Mira dying on the birthday rooftop, or leaving it alone while Elias misremembers: **retired**. They leave together.
- A confirmed unreliable-memory or fabricated Mira-POV twist: **not canon**. Revisiting truthful memories with new context is only a possible technique.
- Mira as secret royalty, a chosen one, or the uprising's uniquely targeted captive: **not established**.
- The entire lower city being ruined or empty, or every upper district being untouched: **false generalizations**.
- Modern post-apocalyptic clothing and the initial age-17 Mira draft: **superseded**.
- Treating every incidental drawing, date, label, architectural feature or image detail as binding: **prohibited**.
