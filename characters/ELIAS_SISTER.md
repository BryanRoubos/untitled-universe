# Mira Varek — Elias's Younger Sister

**Name:** Mira Varek — confirmed  
**Age:** Died at 15, about two years before the present story.  
**Status:** Name and age confirmed; core relationship dynamic confirmed; independent life to be developed

## Facial concept studies — archived (15 October 2026)
Two graphite-style portrait assets were uploaded for her early facial design:
- [Graphite Portrait Studies on Aged Paper](../assets/images/Graphite%20Portrait%20Studies%20on%20Aged%20Paper.png): simple side and frontal portraits.
- [Graphite Portrait Study Sheet](../assets/images/Graphite%20Portrait%20Study%20Sheet.png): a larger multi-angle study with facial feature details.

**Visual direction under exploration:** a youthful, expressive, lightly freckled face; untidy dark hair pulled back with loose strands; believable pencil construction and uneven hatching rather than polished, photorealistic finish. These are selected concept studies, **not confirmation of final appearance, hairstyle, or clothing**. Her design should feel related to Elias without simply copying his features. Both artworks appear in the concept book and can be enlarged.

## Personality
Lively, outgoing, social, curious and eager to find joy beyond survival. She has friends and places of her own. She loves Elias but resents being treated as a child to be protected rather than a person who can choose.

## Childhood
She and Elias grew up without dependable parental care (precise circumstances open). Elias assumed a parental role too young. Their community included other vulnerable children, danger, friendship, mischief and small moments of warmth.

## Relationship with Elias
She repeatedly invites him into life beyond constant vigilance. He loves her fiercely, but his fear can become controlling. Their bond should include jokes, ordinary arguments, warmth and frustration, not just tragedy.

## Disappearance — working premise
On her birthday, Mira and Elias argue about his protectiveness but leave the rooftop together. Later, during the uprising Elias unknowingly helps enable, Mira is captured with other civilians. She survives the initial chaos and dies later. The exact circumstances and timing of her death remain open.

## Important writing rule
Develop her personal aspirations, friendships, values, fears and agency before treating the disappearance as a plot mechanism.

## Open
Final appearance, individual friendships, talents, goals, and what she wanted from the rooftop remain open. Mira’s death is confirmed; its exact circumstances and timing remain open. Her parents’ whereabouts and fate are unknown.

## Journal — confirmed sibling event (17 October 2026)

Elias's weathered leather journal is deeply personal. Mira secretly took it and read some of his private thoughts while she was alive, leaving a small drawing or message in its pages. Elias only discovers that she read it **after the uprising**, when he finds her contribution. This is a private, emotionally complicated boundary-crossing, not a clue that resolves her fate. The message's exact words, the drawing, what she read and the discovery scene are **open**. See [Elias's Personal Items](ELIAS_VAREK_ITEMS.md).

## Confirmed keepsake — silver pendant

Mira owned a small silver pendant. Elias has it after her disappearance, and it is the only thing he has left of her. Where she got it, precisely how it reached him, and its final design remain open.
