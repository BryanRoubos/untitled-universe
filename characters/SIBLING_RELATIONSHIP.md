# Elias and His Sister — Relationship Bible

**Status:** Confirmed emotional core; event specifics working.

Elias was a child forced to act as a guardian. His younger sister was one of the few people who could pull him out of mere survival and make him notice beauty. He saw danger everywhere; she saw possibility. Neither was simply right or wrong.

## Everyday dynamics
- He monitors exits and threats; she introduces him to people.
- He says 'no' reflexively; she challenges him, sometimes fairly and sometimes recklessly.
- Their humor and affection are lived-in, not speeches about their love.
- Her independence is a legitimate need, not a narrative mistake.

## Defining memory
At a bustling market, she persuades a reluctant Elias to climb to a rooftop. She shows him a view beyond the crowded, harsh streets. A possible line: **'You never look up, do you?'** (proposed wording, not locked dialogue).

## The wound
They fight about his protectiveness, then leave the rooftop together, still upset. Mira is later captured during the uprising Elias unknowingly helps enable and dies after the initial chaos. Elias’s guilt centers on being unable to reach her and on his unwitting role in the catastrophe—not on leaving her alone on the rooftop.

## Emotional goal
Their story asks whether love can become control, whether guilt can be mistaken for responsibility, and whether Elias can someday choose life for reasons beyond saving her.
