# Art Direction and References

**Status:** Tone, Elias's Concept 03 face and clothing direction approved; Mira's portrait studies and world details exploratory.

## Desired feeling
Cinematic, tactile, atmospheric dark fantasy. Dense forests and ruined architecture contrast with human warmth, inviting lights, markets, weather and beautiful vistas. Mystery should be visually suggestive, not constantly explicit.

## Authorship and process credit
Original pencil concepts and creative direction: **Bryan Roubos** (as described by the creator). The digital pencil-style studies displayed in this repository, and their subsequent color, refinement, and material illustrations, were generated with AI assistance. Credit the original creative work without presenting the AI-generated digital images themselves as physical hand-drawn originals.

## Visual production style — October 15 feedback
Concept art should resemble actual human-made pencil studies, sketchbook work or thoughtful digital painting, not a glossy, uniform AI illustration. Favor expressive line weight, believable draftsmanship, imperfect pencil/ink marks, restrained color, and purposeful visual exploration. **Do not place generator instructions, such as "no AI look" or "usable for 3D reference," as text on artwork.** Avoid unnecessary annotations; only use natural in-world or design-relevant notes if genuinely useful.

## Small-step visual production workflow
Work on **one component and one decision at a time**, saving approved outcomes to Markdown and the public concept book:
1. **Face:** first rough graphite pencil exploration → refined/colored concept. **Completed; Concept 03 approved.**
2. **Clothing:** first rough pencil outfit exploration → clean/refined clothing sketch → color and fabric/material texture study. **Completed: pencil and textured studies approved.** Draw the clothes, not repeat hair, faces, facial expressions, or complete character boards.
3. **Existing items:** independently sketch each documented object (e.g. pendant, ring, journal) → refine its shape → colors/materials/textures; one item at a time.
4. **Consolidated character sheet:** combine only approved face, body, clothing and items.
5. **Technical 3D references:** clean aligned front, side and back views, then consider modeling/rigging/UE5.

Keep artwork minimal and natural, with few or no labels. Separate rough sketches from textured/color work. Do not treat a generated exploration as canon without the creator's approval. The repo is the source of truth; **read existing character and art docs before suggesting new design decisions**.

## Approved atmospheric city concept — 17 October 2026

Reference: [Lone Adventurer Overlooking the Twilight Citadel](../Lone%20Adventurer%20Overlooking%20the%20Twilight%20Citadel.png) (concept art, not a final map). Creator-approved **visual direction**: a massive connected vertical city with dramatic sunlight on the upper districts, much dimmer lower neighborhoods, damp and dirty masonry, moss, weathering, grime and pockets of warm artificial light. The contrast is beautiful from afar and unpleasant up close. The seated figure's filthy rooftop is a strong material reference.

**Not canonized from the image:** exact buildings, bridges, proportions, spatial openness, population density or an empty undercity. The concept currently feels too open and lacks people. Future street-level studies should show cramped, densely layered, inhabited lower streets and **lively markets**, traders, crowds, stalls and areas with very little visible sky. Preserve the city's life as well as its neglect.

## The Arch coin — established concept canon (17 October 2026)

**Canon status:** The city uses a bronze coin known as an **Arch**. Its defining visual direction—a worn bronze coin bearing the city's architectural arch motif—is established canon. The concept board below records the approved core direction; it is not a claim that every scratch, relief line, scale note or presentation detail is immutable.

### Established visual direction
A modest, everyday bronze coin, worn through ordinary handling. Its central architectural arch connects the coin to the city's monumental arches, layered masonry and interconnected structures. The coin should feel like a familiar object used in markets and daily exchanges, not a pristine commemorative medal or a miniature map of the city. Keep future refinements consistent with the established illustrated concept-art style of *Where the Light Falls*: painterly, tactile and atmospheric rather than photorealistic.

### Details still open
The wider currency system has not been defined. The denominations, relative values, other coin types, exchange relationships and any broader minting or monetary history remain undecided. Small refinements to the coin's exact execution may be made, but should preserve the recognizable bronze Arch concept unless the creator deliberately revises it.
