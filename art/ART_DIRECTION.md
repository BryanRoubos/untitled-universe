# Art Direction and References

**Status:** Tone and Elias's visual baseline confirmed; final style bible open.

## Desired feeling
Cinematic, tactile, atmospheric dark fantasy. Dense forests and ruined architecture contrast with human warmth, inviting lights, markets, weather and beautiful vistas. Mystery should be visually suggestive, not constantly explicit.

## Elias
See [character sheet](../characters/ELIAS_VAREK.md). An earlier reference image was approved in conversation but is **not yet archived** here. Re-create or upload source references before claiming any image is authoritative.

## 3D workflow
1. Assemble and approve consistent character views.
2. Evaluate realistic model-generation/sculpting options against cost and quality.
3. Produce first recognizable Elias model.
4. Inspect likeness and topology before committing to rigging/animation.
5. Integrate into UE5 in a later step.

## Open
Stylization versus photorealism, color language, regional architecture, materials, costume adaptation for historical fantasy, and asset provenance.

## Asset hygiene
Record creator, license, original URL and allowed uses for every third-party asset. Do not commit huge generated assets or UE project caches to this Markdown repository by default.
