## Character design workflow — agreed October 15
Develop Elias Varek in small, reviewable stages:
1. **Face exploration** — several genuine-looking hand-drawn/digital pencil studies of the same young Elias (approximately 23), exploring facial structure, hair, tired brown eyes and optional eyebrow scar. Refine and approve his identity before moving on.
2. **Body** — establish believable proportions (approximately 180 cm), silhouette and posture.
3. **Clothing** — refine historical dark-fantasy layers, colors and materials.
4. **Props** — explore personal objects, bags and accessories, only confirming details the creator approves.
5. **Consolidated character concept board** — assemble the chosen designs.
6. **Technical 3D reference sheets** — only after design approval, produce accurate and consistent front, side and back views for modeling.

Update the relevant Markdown documentation at each approved stage and gradually add the artwork to the website. Preserve early explorations as explorations; never silently promote generated choices to canon.
