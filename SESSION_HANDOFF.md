## Most recent world decision — connected city
The creator confirmed that the monumental, sunlit upper districts in the title artwork and the inhabited lower districts in the market/viaduct concept belong to **one enormous, physically connected, vertically layered city**. The creator uploaded `Where the Light Falls.png` as a panoramic architectural concept showing upper, middle and lower levels; it is displayed on the website. The image is **not a precise map** and its labels (government, nobility, cathedrals, waterfalls, workshops, etc.) do not establish lore. City history, political structure, class relations, access routes and individual architecture details remain open. *Arcane* is a reference for social complexity and spatial storytelling, not a world to copy.
